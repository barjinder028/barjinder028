<h2 align="center">💠 About Me</h2>
<p align="center">

</p>
<p align="center">
For 7+ years I worked across <b>cloud operations</b> (Azure, KQL, SLA analytics), <b>QA engineering</b> (test automation, root-cause analysis on ML-integrated systems), and <b>data operations</b>. In January 2026 I made a deliberate call to specialize in <b>Agentic AI</b> — since then I've earned certifications from <b>Oracle</b> and <b>IBM</b>, and I've been building hands-on with <b>LangChain</b>, the <b>OpenAI Agents SDK</b>, <b>Model Context Protocol (MCP)</b>, and <b>RAG pipelines</b>. My QA and cloud-ops background gives me a head start on the discipline agentic systems actually need — evaluation, guardrails, and reliability, not just prompting. Currently building a portfolio of agentic AI projects and looking for my next role as an <b>Agentic AI / LLM Engineer</b>.
</p>
